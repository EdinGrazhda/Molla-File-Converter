The two campaign designs were created with the built-in image-generation tool.
They are **promotional illustrations**, not screenshots or proof of native blur.
The icon is an editable project-native SVG, with a PNG embedded in the app.
The supplied image-generation prompts are in [SOCIAL-PROMPTS.md](SOCIAL-PROMPTS.md).

Use the square design without cropping for feed posts. Use the landscape design
where a wide image fits. Keep headlines and apple edges inside the crop when adapting
to another platform. These are not platform-specific upload templates; check
the destination's preview before posting.

## Posting notes

- Add your real GitHub link when it exists; there is no release/download URL yet.
- Do not claim iOS availability or guaranteed iPhone-style blur. Molla is a Linux app;
  blur depends on the desktop compositor.
- Show an actual screenshot or short recording alongside the artwork when demonstrating behavior.
- Credit the Nashi engine when discussing implementation.
- Avoid “supports every file” or “lossless for all formats.” Conversion varies by format.
- Alt text, square: “Molla wordmark above a luminous glass apple with an M monogram on a dark teal background.”
- Alt text, wide: “Molla with four glass apples in red, green, yellow and gray, and the headline Your files. A fresh form.”
